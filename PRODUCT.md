# Product

Directory is a private, single-owner **personal digital home**: one place
for the day's plan (tasks, calendar, reminders, projects, habits), writing
(notes, journal), media (library, music), money (budget), and JARVIS, the
assistant that turns plain sentences into previewed, undoable changes.

- **Who:** one owner, on a desktop browser and an iPhone (installed PWA).
  No teams, no sharing, no ads, no growth loops.
- **Job:** answer three questions at a glance — *what is happening today,
  what needs my attention, and what can I do next?* — and let the owner act
  on the answer in one or two steps.
- **Constraints:** $0/month (Cloudflare free tier), passkey auth, every
  record scoped to the signed-in owner, works offline with a sync outbox,
  no third-party embeds or paid APIs, no bank connections.
- **Mode:** Operate. Scanability, consistency, keyboard speed, and calm
  outrank expression. Personality lives in details: the printed-planner
  grid, paper grain, the turntable, quiet streak acknowledgements.
- **Voice:** plain, short, sentence case. Say what happened and what to do
  next. No exclamation marks, no jokes in errors, no fake data presented
  as real.
